Decision (resolve, v0.1.0, R18; Red Team F18). A node's templates are ranked alternative geometries of one node (unique id and rank). A template's components are the species of a separated state, each a MolecularState with a stoichiometric count. All templates of a node share total charge (sum count x charge) and element multiset. The template's atom index space is the components' atoms concatenated in order, each once. Edges: cycles allowed (catalytic cycles), self-loops and duplicate pairs rejected. Graph files carry schema_version: 1. Why: one meaning per field for v0.6.0-v0.8.0 consumers.
