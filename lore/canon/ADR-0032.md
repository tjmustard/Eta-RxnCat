Constrained-core pre-relaxation freezes the core; it never restrains it and never combines frozen atoms with a dihedral constraint. mapping.relax.relax_constrained_core calls Optimizer.minimize(state, engine, frozen_atom_indices=core, options=...) with no dihedral constraints (v0.3.0 audit 04: frozen atoms plus a dihedral constraint hung in 2 of 4 seeds), refuses an options key "constraints" up front, propagates engine and optimizer errors unchanged, and raises CoreDisplacementError when any core atom moves more than 1e-8 A (max per-atom displacement, no superposition). The result is a pre-relaxed TS guess, not a minimum, and is never ground-state checked. TJ: "Q2. (b)". Source: missions/release-0.4.0 R6, R7.
