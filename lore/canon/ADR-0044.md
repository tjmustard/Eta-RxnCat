Context: red team F2 — keeping two re-converged copies of one saddle when |ΔG| ≥ 0.05 kcal/mol lowers the pathway G by RT ln k (0.41 kcal/mol for k = 2). Resolve measured the duplicate noise floor on Tier 4: |ΔG| ≤ 0.0021 kcal/mol, RMSD ≤ 4e-5 Å (HF-11).
Decision (mission release-0.6.0 R2, R8, R17): two valid TS ensemble members are one if their proper-rotation RMSD with H (seed perceived-graph automorphisms) is below 0.125 Å and their face tuples are equal; the lower-G member is kept, every merge records ΔG and RMSD, and a merge with |ΔG| > 0.05 kcal/mol is flagged but still merged. Never sum two members that share a shape. The pathway G is −RT ln Σ g_i exp(−G_i/RT) (log-sum-exp) over members within 3.0 kcal/mol of the lowest; others are recorded outside_window. Each member carries its degeneracy (1 per ADR-0036).
