Thermochemistry is derived, never stored: the species runner stores the frequency stage's Hessian and energy, and `ThermoResult` is recomputed from it with the requested `ThermoConfig` and `ThermoAnalysisOptions`, so changing T or omega_0 needs no engine call and the store never holds two thermo answers for one Hessian. The numpy/RDKit math lives in `eta_rxncat.thermo` (core stays data-only), with its own CODATA 2018 constants module checked against `engines.units` within 1e-12 relative rather than editing the engines node. Symmetry numbers come from capped graph automorphisms (explicit `maxMatches`; the cap raises, never a silent sigma). Source: missions/release-0.3.0 R1, R8, R9, R15; slice 03.
