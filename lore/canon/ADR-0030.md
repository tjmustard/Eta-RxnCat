What a graft refuses and what it records (v0.4.0). A graft raises GraftClashError only when two product atoms are closer than min_distance_angstrom (0.5 A, keyed). Close contacts — kept-template x appended pairs not bonded in the graft's source graph and closer than 1.3 x the sum of RDKit covalent radii (DetermineConnectivity's own covFactor) — are recorded, never raised. Junction handedness is recorded as a signed volume at each template-appended junction bond, using the two lowest-index kept template neighbours. On a TS fixture (M1), the pre-relaxed product is gated on perceived connectivity equal to the reference TS's and on junction signs equal and nonzero; full-molecule RMSDs are reported, not gated. TJ: "P1 A / P2 A" and "R1 B, R2 A". Measured on M1: four contacts (two C...H at 1.12 A, two C...C at 1.96 A) that perception alone would partly miss (it gives each H one partner). Source: missions/release-0.4.0 R1, R16, R22.
