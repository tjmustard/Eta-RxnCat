Context: ADR-0037 records TJ Q-TJ3 (GFN2 gates convergence and the mode count only). TJ later chose v0.5.0 Q-TJ4 "A (Recommended)", which also gates the identity core on GFN2; ADR-0037 is stale (red team F14).
Decision (supersedes ADR-0037; mission release-0.6.0 R14, from release-0.5.0 R32): on GFN2 a TS is gated on convergence, exactly one imaginary mode below the A2 cutoff, the active-core KE fraction ≥ 0.60, a forming-stretch overlap ≥ 0.50 and in-phase forming bonds; the full identity (including the distance window), connectivity and numeric ΔG‡/ΔΔG‡ windows are reported, not gated. On UMA all of them are gated, plus the windows. GFN2 sign/ordering checks are gated per pair only where measured to reproduce (TJ v0.6.0 Q3 A); a pair TJ marks report-only (v0.5.0 TS4a) stays report-only.
