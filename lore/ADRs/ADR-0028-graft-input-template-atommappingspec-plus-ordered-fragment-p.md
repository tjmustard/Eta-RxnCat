Graft input convention (v0.4.0). A graft is one call: graft(template, fragment, GraftSpec). The template side reuses ADR-0005's AtomMappingSpec unchanged (keep/delete partition; alignment is the ordered template side of the pairs, ⊆ keep, ≥ 3; anchors must be empty in v0.4.0). The fragment side is an ordered `fragment_alignment` list, paired by position. Paired fragment atoms are always dropped (their template partners take their place); an optional `fragment_delete` (empty by default, disjoint from the pairs) removes further fragment atoms. The product is the template keep atoms in keep order, coordinates copied bitwise, followed by the remaining fragment atoms in ascending fragment index at their superposed coordinates. Pair elements must match. Product charge and multiplicity are always explicit; the whole-input charge sum is reported only. TJ: "Q4. A", "Q5. A", and on the clean-room nMAP comparison "R1 B, R2 A" (R1 B = optional incoming delete list, paired atoms always dropped). Divergences from nMAP (0-based, template-first, no range syntax, refusals) are listed as CR-01..CR-15 in docs/prd/v0.4.0/DISCREPANCIES.md. Source: missions/release-0.4.0 R15, R17, R10, R11.
