Context: red team F1 (blocker) — endo and exo Diels–Alder TSs share their forming bonds, and the pinned active core [0..5] omits the dienophile CH₂, so a TS conformer sample can cross faces, re-converge to the other diastereomer's TS and pass the identity gate. The red team's plane descriptor (CH₂ side of the diene plane) does not separate them (resolve HF-3: +2.86 vs +2.60 Å).
Decision (mission release-0.6.0 R1): a TS ensemble member is summed only if its relative configuration across every forming bond equals its seed's. Per forming bond (i, j): s = sign V_i · sign V_j, V_c = det[x_a − x_c, x_b − x_c, x_p − x_c], with (a, b) the two lowest-index neighbours of c in the seed's perceived graph other than its partner p. Mirror- and rigid-motion-invariant; flips between diastereomers. Measured on Liu's TSs: endo (+1, +1), exo (−1, −1). A mismatch is excluded as face_mismatch and any face_mismatch in Tier 4 fails the acceptance record. The sampler anchors each fragment by its core atoms plus, when those are fewer than three or collinear, their heavy neighbours, so it cannot cross faces by construction.
