Decision (resolve, v0.7.1 R3/R26; Red Team RT-06). In tests/integration/** an AST scan fails on: unittest.mock; any monkeypatch attribute (by name and alias) other than setenv, delenv, chdir; assignment, augassign or del to an Attribute/Subscript rooted at an imported eta_rxncat module or sys.modules; builtin setattr/delattr; imports from tests.* other than tests.integration; and the gpu, mlip_mace, mlip_uma, slow markers. Engines come only from eta's registry via build_engine. One negative case per form.
Trade-off: rejected a naming convention or review-only rule (unenforced). Chosen: a tested scanner, at the cost of upkeep when a new bypass form appears.
