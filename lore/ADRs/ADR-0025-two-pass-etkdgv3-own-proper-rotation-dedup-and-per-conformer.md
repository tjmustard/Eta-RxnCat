Conformer pools come from two ETKDGv3 passes: the standard one and a second with `useExpTorsionAnglePrefs=False` (keyed `torsion_free_pass`, default true), because the experimental torsion preferences never sample the s-cis-side class of conjugated dienes (0/40 for 1,3-dimethoxybutadiene, 0/50 for butadiene; the second pass gives 24/40 and 26/50, measured by resolve, and the class survives GFN2 optimization). Deduplication is the project's own (proper-rotation Kabsch over graph automorphisms with H, never `pruneRmsThresh`, never a reflection). Mirror degeneracy is a property of each conformer: g = 2 iff the molecule is graph-achiral and the conformer is chiral; a mirror partner record is dropped. Seed geometries must match the SMILES's connectivity and stereo (`useChirality=True` plus canonical isomeric SMILES equality) and are never removed by pre-dedup. Source: missions/release-0.3.0 R3, R21, R24, R28; slice 05.
