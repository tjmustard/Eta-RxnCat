Decision (resolve, v0.7.1 R35; v0.7.0 slice 10 carry-forward). run_selectivity runs only priority-1 pathways and treats priority >= 2 as downstream species; the v0.7.0 enumeration plan puts its TS nodes at priority 2 so the fallback runner treats same-priority TS nodes as competitors. Both contracts stand. The integration test builds its run graph from the loaded candidate graph with model_copy, setting only the two TS nodes to priority 1, and says so in its docstring; pathway inputs come from enumerated_pathway_inputs on the unmodified candidate. The join of the two conventions is deferred to v0.8.0's graph-to-TS scheduler and listed as a known issue.
Trade-off: rejected changing either contract in a quality release. Relates to ADR-0046 and ADR-0052; contradicts no CANON node.
