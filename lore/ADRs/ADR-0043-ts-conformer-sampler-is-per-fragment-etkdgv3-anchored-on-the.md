Context: TJ Q1 B asks for core-frozen TS conformer sampling. The draft's sampler (ETKDGv3 with a coordMap pinning the core on the two-fragment TS graph) was measured by resolve (HF-4): RDKit 2026.09.2 ignores the map across disconnected fragments (core off by up to 2.15 Å after alignment, 2/20 samples on the seed's face, useRandomCoords embeds 0/20).
Decision (mission release-0.6.0 R16, R24): sample per connected fragment — ETKDGv3 on the fragment alone (numThreads=1, randomSeed) with a coordMap on its anchor atoms, Kabsch-placed on the seed by the anchor — plus seeded Cartesian perturbations of the non-core atoms (σ 0.3 Å); reset the core to the seed's coordinates exactly; pre-dedup at 0.125 Å with perceived-graph automorphisms. Defaults n_embed 10, n_perturb 10, max_research 10, all keyed. Sampling is deterministic and not cached; pre-relaxation is cached under the existing constrained_preopt stage kind with frozen_atom_indices = the core (no new stage kind). Measured: core ≤ 0.011 Å before reset, 50/50 faces kept (HF-5).
